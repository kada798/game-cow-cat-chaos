## Registration and Runtime

The builder keeps the kneading body and both front paws on the same 512 x 512 canvas as the frozen pose, with [256, 448] support and 112px normalized head span. Kneading moves only the paw layers in opposition and softly compresses the independent teddy head. The bed's front rim and mattress have identical canvases; the front rim naturally occludes the plush's lower outline. The family comes to watch before the controller freezes the cat, stops its animation-frame callback and allows an explicit transition to the ending. No countdown, punishment or injury is involved. `tests/teddy.cjs` checks alpha contact, stationary final pixels, state order, cancellation, mute/replay and viewport layout.
